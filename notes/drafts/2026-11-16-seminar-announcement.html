Sampling More Strategically But Just as Flexibly as IID

  • Speaker: Fred Hickernell (Illinois Tech)
  • When: Monday, November 16, 2026, 1:50–2:50 PM America/Chicago (CST)
  • Where: RE 104

Many scientific calculations estimate an average using independent, identically distributed (IID) samples. Low-discrepancy sampling spreads points more evenly and can give more accurate answers for the same computational effort. I will discuss recent research by our group on methods that retain the freedom to add samples as needed, demonstrate how QMCPy makes these ideas available for practical simulation, and show how AI is accelerating our research and software development.
